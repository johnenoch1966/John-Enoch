  2. Unstructured launch without continuous workflow habit reinforcementInitial novelty quickly gives way to disuse when employees receive software without targeted role-specific workflows. [187][188][249]
  3. Assuming per-seat time savings automatically yields P&L returnScattered five-minute savings across decentralized workers rarely aggregate into defensible cost takeout or measurable headcount efficiency. [271][356][506]
